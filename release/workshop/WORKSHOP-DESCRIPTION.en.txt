[h1]Bring your music into the Constant[/h1]
Listen to your NetEase Cloud Music and QQ Music playlists while exploring, building, sailing or venturing into the caves.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Sign in by QR code, browse playlists and search, with playback, seek, volume, queue and repeat controls. Accounts are separate; switching browsing sources does not interrupt the current song.
[*]Floating lyrics, album artwork, song cards and a compact player, with adjustable lyrics, hotkeys, interface and top-left button.
[*]Regional and rain spatial effects process the music. Closing the DST client pauses playback; resume manually after reopening.
[*]Version checks read GitHub Releases; check manually or disable automatic startup and six-hour checks. Updates are not installed automatically. Steam updates Workshop subscriptions.
[/list]
[b]Client-only mod: your server and friends do not need to install it. Supports 64-bit Windows 10/11.[/b]

[h2]First-time setup[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribe, wait for Steam to download the mod, open its Workshop folder and double-click 一键启动播放器.cmd (One-click Start Player) at the root. The included companion is checked, extracted, connected and started automatically.
[*]Enable the mod under Client Mods, enter a world and press F8. If the game already loaded older Lua, reload it once after updating; use ESC → Music Player: it opens music settings when online, or the connection page and reloads the connection when offline.
[*]Choose NetEase or QQ Music on the account page and confirm the QR login with the corresponding phone app. Later, double-click the launcher again, or use 自启动设置.cmd (Startup Settings) to opt in to starting it on Windows sign-in. Startup is off by default.
[/list]
The first launch requires a double-click: the game cannot launch the local program from scratch. To stop it, run 停止配套播放器.cmd (Stop Companion Player) in windows-player. If the opened mod folder is obscured, use Alt+Tab. Existing manual installations are not migrated automatically; a new QR sign-in may be needed. The Workshop folder is usually SteamLibrary\steamapps\workshop\content\322330\3836261433 or mods\workshop-3836261433 inside the game folder.

[h2]Beta notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribing does not start the companion. Platform membership, licensing, region and preview restrictions apply; some QQ songs playable on a phone may not return a playback URL through the interface.
[*]Word timing for ordinary line-timed lyrics is estimated; accurate timing requires word timestamps. Glass styling uses translucent layers and highlights, not background blur.
[*]Local audio imports, synchronized listening across players and Mac/Linux are not supported.
[/list]
